Module 1: Organisational Context & Approach

Cynefin Framework (Snowden)

Simple/Obvious: Stable; Best Practice; Sense-Categorise-Respond.

Complicated: Knowable with experts; Good Practice; Sense-Analyse-Respond.

Complex: Emergent; Safe-to-fail experiments; Probe-Sense-Respond.

Chaotic: Crisis/no patterns; Act-Sense-Respond.

Planned vs Emergent Change

Planned has defined endpoints and structured control. Emergent is experimental, continuous, and co-designed with front-line staff.

Kelman's Three Levels of Adoption

1. Compliance: To earn rewards or avoid penalties (surface level).
2. Identification: To belong or maintain respect for leaders.
3. Internalization: Deep value alignment; becomes self-sustaining.

Module 2: People and Change

Conscious Competence Learning Curve

1. Unconscious Incompetence
2. Conscious Incompetence (Learning Dip): Friction, slow execution, confusion.
3. Conscious Competence: Requires deliberate effort.
4. Unconscious Competence: Second nature mastery.

Beckhard & Harris Formula

D × V × F > R

Dissatisfaction × Vision × First Steps must exceed Resistance. Because factors multiply, if any is 0, resistance prevails.

Edgar Schein: Survival vs Learning Anxiety

Change occurs when Survival Anxiety > Learning Anxiety. Leaders must lower learning anxiety through Psychological Safety and training, rather than only scaring teams.

Module 3: Leadership, Teams & Culture

Lencioni's 5 Dysfunctions (Bottom Up)

1. Trust → 2. Constructive Conflict → 3. Commitment → 4. Mutual Accountability → 5. Focus on Team Results (apex follows all others).

Carolyn Taylor Culture Mechanisms

Symbols: How leaders spend time, promote, or allocate funds.
Behaviours: Day-to-day actions and role-modeling.
Systems: Formal policies, KPIs, IT tools, and incentives.

Roles Distinction

• Business Leader: Owns benefits realization.
• Sponsor: Owns strategic vision & budget.
• Line Leader: Owns local WIIFM impact communication.
• Change Manager: Facilitates and structures human enablement.

Exam Strategy Golden Rules

1. Multi-statement questions: Check statement 1 and statement 2 independently before picking options.

2. Sender of messages: Sponsor sends strategic vision; Line Manager sends personal/WIIFM context.

3. Benefits: Change managers never own benefits; Business Leaders do.
